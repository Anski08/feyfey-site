Privacy

What FeyFey stores, in specifics.

This page describes the actual behaviour of the software, field by field, rather than the broadest permissions a lawyer would prefer we reserve.

Draft — not yet a binding legal document

FeyFey has not yet finalised its operating entity or governing jurisdiction. Passages in [BRACKETS] are unresolved. The factual descriptions of what the software stores are accurate as of October 2026; the legal framing has not been reviewed by a qualified professional. This page is published early in the interest of transparency, not as a contract.

The short version

Your video files are never uploaded. We store the email address you sign up with, the text you write about your videos, and your review schedule. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Never leaves your device: your video files, and their contents.

Stored on our behalf: your email, your library text, your schedule, your subscription status.

Not collected at all: analytics, behavioural tracking, advertising identifiers, location, contacts.

1. Who is responsible

FeyFey is operated by [ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. For any question about this page or your data, contact [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What we store

2.1 Account information

  • The email address you register with.
  • A password, stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider. FeyFey never receives or stores your password in readable form.
  • A generated account identifier, and the timestamps of account creation and last sign-in.

2.2 Your library and schedule

For each video you add, the following is stored on our behalf:

  • a title;
  • a subject and a priority you assign;
  • an optional question you write to answer before watching;
  • your bookmarks — the label text you type and the timestamp in seconds it refers to;
  • scheduling state — the date last reviewed, the next review date, the current interval, the ease factor and the number of reviews;
  • your chosen daily goal.

A detail most policies would omit

When you add a video, its title is taken from the filename by default. Filenames can be revealing. The title is stored on our behalf, so if a filename contains something you would not want recorded, rename the item in the library — the stored title follows what you set.

2.3 Subscription information

  • An identifier issued by our payment processor, linking your account to your customer record with them.
  • Your subscription status and the date the current period ends.

FeyFey never receives, handles or stores card numbers. Payment details are entered on the payment processor's own hosted pages.

3. What we never receive

  • Your video files. They are written to your browser's local storage on the device that added them and are never transmitted to us. We hold no copy and cannot produce one — including in response to a legal request.
  • The contents of your videos. Nothing is transcribed, analysed, scanned or used to train any model.
  • Analytics or behavioural data. The application loads no analytics script, sets no advertising or tracking cookie, and builds no profile of your activity.
  • Location, contacts, camera or microphone data, except that any video you choose to add is handled exactly as described above — locally.

5. Why we are allowed to hold it

We process your account information and library because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for, and your subscription information because it is necessary to run a paid service and to meet record-keeping obligations. We do not rely on advertising or profiling as a basis for anything, because we do neither.

6. How long we keep it

Your account and library are kept for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, the associated records are removed. Payment records may be retained by our payment processor for the period required by [JURISDICTION] financial record-keeping rules.

Your video files are not covered by any of this, because they are on your device. Deleting them is a matter of removing them in the app or clearing your browser data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or object to its processing. To exercise any of these, contact [CONTACT EMAIL]. We will respond within [RESPONSE PERIOD].

A current limitation, stated plainly

FeyFey does not yet offer a self-service export or account deletion button. Until it does, these requests are handled manually by email. We would rather say that than imply tooling that does not exist.

8. Security

Access to your records is enforced at the database level: every row carries an account identifier, and the database refuses to return rows that do not belong to the requesting account. Subscription status is writable only by our server, in response to cryptographically signed events from the payment processor.

No system is perfect. Should a breach occur that affects your data, we will notify affected users and any required authority as obliged under [JURISDICTION] law.

9. Children

FeyFey is not directed at children under [AGE], and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes

If this page changes materially, the date below changes with it, and we will notify signed-in users. Until the draft notice at the top of this page is removed, treat this document as a good-faith description of current behaviour rather than a finalised policy.
